About Us

Welcome to Travalgar Square, Reitzpark’s ultimate destination for flavor, fuel, and fantastic company! Located right in the vibrant heart of Welkom, Free State, we are a proud local hangout where hunger meets its match. We specialized in bridging the gap between local street-style fast food and premium grilled selections—making us the go-to spot whether you are treating the family, grabbing a quick lunch, or chilling out over the weekend.

Our menu is built on simple, perfectly executed crowd-pleasers. From our signature golden, freshly cut chips to our expertly seasoned, flame-grilled meats packed with smoky local flavor, we use high-quality ingredients to deliver a meal that hits the spot every single time. At Travalgar Square, we believe that food is about more than just eating—it’s about community, culture, and good energy. Stop by our local spot today, catch the aroma of our open grills, and experience the best taste Welkom has to offer!
